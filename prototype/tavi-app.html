What this means
Tonight's HRV is within your normal range. A higher HRV usually points to better recovery, but it's personal — TAVI compares you to your own baseline, not to other people.
How it is measured

HRV is the variation in time between heartbeats, measured overnight during sleep — it is not your heart rate. When there is no valid overnight measurement, TAVI shows -- ms (unavailable), never 0. A change in method or source stays visible on your history.
